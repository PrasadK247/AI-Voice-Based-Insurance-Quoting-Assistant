# Demo Script – UC189

## Duration: 10 minutes

### Opening (1 min)
"Welcome to UC189 – the AI Voice-Based Insurance Quoting Assistant.
This solution helps customers get personalized insurance quotes
through natural voice conversations."

### Demo Flow (7 min)

**Step 1 – Voice Input**
- Click the microphone button on the dashboard
- Speak: "Hi, I'm looking for health insurance for my family of 4"
- Show the real-time transcription and sound wave visualizer

**Step 2 – AI Conversation**
- AI responds with voice: "I'd be happy to help! Can you tell me your age, approximate annual income, and any pre-existing conditions?"
- Answer the profiling questions via voice or preset scenario
- Show the customer profile being built in real-time

**Step 3 – Risk Assessment**
- Show the risk score calculation and risk category (Low, Medium, High)
- Explain factors: age, pre-existing conditions, smoking status, family size

**Step 4 – Policy Recommendations**
- AI presents top recommended policies matching budget and coverage needs
- Voice explanation of each option
- Show comparison table and benefits breakdown

**Step 5 – Quote Generation**
- Select a policy (e.g. Family Care Plus or Premium Health Shield)
- Show detailed premium breakdown (Monthly vs. Annual with 5% discount)
- Interactive deductible slider and optional add-ons (Dental, Vision)

**Step 6 – Call Summary & Uniphore Intelligence**
- Show the auto-generated call summary
- Highlight Uniphore sentiment analysis (Positive, Neutral, Negative)
- Show conversation intelligence insights: speech tempo, engagement, tone

### Technical Architecture (1 min)
- Show the LangGraph workflow diagram
- Explain agent orchestration (Customer Profiling -> Risk -> Recommendation -> Quote)
- Highlight Uniphore, Whisper, ElevenLabs, and Azure OpenAI integration points

### Closing (1 min)
"This solution reduces quote generation time by 70%,
improves customer satisfaction, and enables 24/7 self-service."
